Capstone Classroom Discussion Prompt

"Look back at all four seasons (Winter, Spring, Summer, Autumn). Which season's celebrations reflect the deepest historical roots, and why?"

Encourage learners to deploy contrasting comparative conjunctions (e.g., whereas, unlike, both, similarly) and seasonal descriptive adjectives.
