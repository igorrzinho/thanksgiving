Grammar Clinic: Expressing Purpose

B1 Mastery • "In order to" vs. "So that"
1. In order to + Verb (Infinitive) Action Focus

Used when the subject performing the main action is the same person or group.

"Thousands of people arrive early in order to watch the festive parade in New York City."
2. So that + Subject + Modal verb Outcome Focus

Common when introducing a different subject or explaining the desired result (often paired with can, could, will).

"Parents hide chocolate eggs carefully so that children can enjoy the morning search."
Pair Work Quick Quiz:

"Chicago dyes its river green ______ visitors can marvel at the tradition."
