🇺🇸 United States
Calendar Window

Christmas in America

Dates Dec 24th – 25th
Core Cultural Customs 4 Essential Practices
1
Family Reunions & Interstate Travel

Millions travel extensive distances across multiple states via road trips and bustling airports to celebrate in multi-generational family homes.

2
Stockings by the Fireplace

Hanging felt knit stockings above the fireplace mantle on Christmas Eve, ready to be stuffed with pocket toys, candy canes, and festive knick-knacks.

3
Milk & Cookies for Santa

Young children thoughtfully set out a plate of freshly baked chocolate-chip cookies and cold milk—alongside carrots for the reindeer—before bedtime.

4
Exterior Holiday Lights & Lawn Displays

Entire neighborhoods decorate rooftops and gardens with thousands of colorful synchronized lights, animated reindeer, and giant inflatable characters.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom
Calendar Window

Christmas & Boxing Day

Dates Dec 25th – 26th
Core Cultural Customs 4 Essential Practices
1
Paper Christmas Crackers

Pulled in pairs at the Christmas dining table; with a sudden crack, they release a colorful tissue paper crown, an amusing novelty toy, and a classic pun riddle.

2
Mince Pies & Flaming Pudding

Indulging in sweet mincemeat pastries spiced with dried fruits, followed by dense steamed Christmas pudding ignited with heated brandy at table service.

3
The King's Christmas Broadcast

A cherished civic tradition where the monarch shares an annual reflective television address to the Commonwealth at precisely 3:00 PM on Christmas Day.

4
Boxing Day (December 26th)

A statutory public bank holiday centered on country walks, visiting distant relations, attending top-tier football fixtures, and shopping seasonal clearance deals.

school Classroom Language Box (B1 Level)

Model Sentence

Key Vocabulary Term Cultural Term

Boxing Day

Historically the day domestic staff received gift boxes and gratitude from employers.
